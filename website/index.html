About

Andrew McDougall is a New Zealand-born author based in Melbourne. He is the co-author of two published non-fiction books and the author of numerous articles and essays. His photography and multimedia work has been exhibited in Sydney and Melbourne.

Current Novel

Under the Bridge, Before the Storm is an 85,000-word work of contemporary literary fiction set in Wellington, Melbourne, Daegu and Kyoto.

Photographer Hamish Cameron is drawn into the world of museum curator Isabella Taylor, whose investigation into a stolen Māori cloak reveals a link to his own family history. When costume designer Claire encounters Hamish's exhibition flyer in Melbourne, all three lives are set on an irreversible course. In a Kyoto museum, a wooden comb leads Hamish and Claire to a bridge and a reckoning with the past.
